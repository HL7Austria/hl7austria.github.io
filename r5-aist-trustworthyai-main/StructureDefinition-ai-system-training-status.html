Extension: AI System-Specific Training Status

Official URL: http://example.org/fhir/trust-ai-transparency/StructureDefinition/ai-system-training-status Version: 0.1.0
Draft as of 2026-11-12 Computable Name: AISystemTrainingStatus

Records whether the practitioner acting in the documented role has completed training specific to the relevant AI system.

Context of Use

    This extension may be used on the following element(s)

    • Element ID: PractitionerRole

Usage info

Usages:

  • Use this Extension: Trust AI Practitioner Role
  • Examples for this Extension: PractitionerRole/dr-practitioner-role and PractitionerRole/practitionerrole-reviewer-001
