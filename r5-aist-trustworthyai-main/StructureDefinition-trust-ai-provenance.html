Resource Profile: Trust AI Provenance

Official URL: http://example.org/fhir/trust-ai-transparency/StructureDefinition/trust-ai-provenance Version: 0.1.0
Draft as of 2026-11-12 Computable Name: Trust_AIProvenance

A Provenance profile linking an AI-generated output to the contributing AI system, source data, and relevant processing or governance context.

Regulatory Requirements

This profile is part of the AI Output Context layer. It implements the following documentation requirements from the Requirements Traceability analysis.

Requirement Law / Source Metadata Field Static / Dynamic
SYS-10 AI Act Art. 12 / EHDS ANNEX II (3) Audit Trail & Access Logging Dynamic
USE-04 GDPR Art. 5(1) Case-Specific Indication Dynamic
LAW-01a GDPR Art. 6(1) Legal Basis (General) Dynamic
LAW-01b GDPR Art. 9(2) Health Data Exception Dynamic
LAW-02 GDPR Art. 22 Automated Decision Flag Dynamic
LAW-03 EHDS Art. 51 / 52 Data Provenance (Primary/Secondary) Dynamic
