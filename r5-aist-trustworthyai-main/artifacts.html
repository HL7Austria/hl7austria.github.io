This page provides a list of the FHIR artifacts defined as part of this implementation guide.

Structures: Resource Profiles

These define constraints on FHIR resources for systems conforming to this implementation guide.

EU AI & EHDS Consent Profile

A profile on the Consent resource to capture GDPR legal basis and EHDS secondary use opt-out.
EU AI Act Compliant Device

A Device profile representing an AI system, fulfilling EU AI Act metadata requirements.
EU AI Act Human Overseer (PractitionerRole)

Defines the qualifications, specialty, and AI-specific training of the human responsible for oversight of the AI system, as required by the AI Act (HL-02).
EU AI Act Human Oversight (HL)

Documents the professional review and clinical decision-making regarding AI outputs.
EU AI Act Machine Execution Audit Event

Logs the automated execution of an AI model, establishing the core traceability chain.
EU AI Act Model Card

The official AI Model Card. It mandates the inclusion of intended purpose, risk assessments, and performance metrics as per the EU AI Act and GDPR.
EU AI Act Observation (Validated Result)

Profile for clinical findings generated by an AI system, implementing LAW-02 (AI Flag) and linking to the AI Device.
EU AI Act Patient Right to Explanation

Documents the fulfillment of the patient's right to a clear and meaningful explanation regarding the AI's role and the clinical decision (LAW-07).
EU AI Act Provenance (Human-in-the-Loop)

Records the execution of an AI system, including human oversight and data provenance.
EU AI Responsible Organization

An organization acting as a manufacturer or deployer (owner) of an AI system, containing required regulatory contacts.

Structures: Extension Definitions

These define constraints on FHIR data types for systems conforming to this implementation guide.

AI Performance Metrics

Captures quantitative metrics and bias disclosures.
AI Privacy Metadata

GDPR and AI Act privacy parameters. Third country transfer flags and data retention policies at the model level.
AI System Specific Training

Mandatory flag indicating whether the human actor has received specific training for the utilized AI tool.
AI Training Data Metadata

Details regarding provenance, EHDS categories, and data quality.
Case-Specific Indication

The clinical reason why the AI was used for this specific patient.
EHDS Data Permit

The unique ID of the Health Data Access Body permit (required if secondary use).
EHDS Usage Category

Categorizes the data processing as Primary Care or Secondary Use according to the EHDS.
EU AI Act Explanation Requested Flag

Flag indicating if the patient (data subject) explicitly requested a clear and meaningful explanation of the AI's role and the clinical decision.
EU AI Act Log Integrity Signature

Cryptographic signature or verification hash to ensure the integrity, accountability, and non-repudiation of the AI execution audit log.
Model Card Reference

A reference to the DocumentReference resource that acts as the Model Card, containing detailed documentation, intended purpose, and risk assessments.
Patient AI Info Provided Flag

Confirmation that the patient was informed about the use of AI systems according to AI Act transparency rules.
Third-Country Data Transfer

Captures if patient data is transferred outside the EU by this device.

Terminology: Value Sets

These define sets of codes used by systems conforming to this implementation guide.

AI Data Quality ValueSet

Attributes describing the quality of training data.
AI Performance Metric ValueSet

Codes for technical quality and performance metrics.
EHDS Data Category ValueSet (Final Regulation 2025/327)

Mandatory categories of electronic health data for secondary use according to Art. 51 EHDS.
EHDS Usage Category ValueSet

Defines whether the data usage is primary care or secondary use.
EU AI Case-Specific Indication ValueSet

Clinical and administrative reasons for AI application.
GDPR Art 6 Legal Basis for AI

Value set including GDPR Article 6 legal bases relevant for documenting the lawful processing of personal data.
GDPR Art 9 Exception for AI Health Data

Value set including GDPR Article 9 exceptions relevant for documenting the processing of special categories of personal data, including health data.
Human Intervention Type ValueSet

Codes representing the type of human oversight or intervention.

Terminology: Code Systems

These define new code systems used by systems conforming to this implementation guide.

EU AI Act Custom Codes

Central repository of legal and technical terms according to the EU AI Act.
GDPR Art 6 Legal Basis

Codes representing legal bases for processing personal data according to GDPR Article 6.
GDPR Art 9 Exceptions

Codes representing exceptions for processing special categories of personal data according to GDPR Article 9.
