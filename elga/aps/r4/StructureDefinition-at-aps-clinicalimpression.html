Resource Profile: AT APS ClinicalImpression

Official URL: https://fhir.hl7.at/elga/aps/r4/StructureDefinition/at-aps-clinicalimpression Version: 1.0.0
Active as of 2026-02-16 Computable Name: AtApsClinicalImpression

Das AT APS-Profil für die ClinicalImpression-Ressource berücksichtigt die österreichischen Vorgaben (z.B. Patient).

Usages:

  • Use this Profile: AT APS Bundle
  • Refer to this Profile: AT APS ClinicalImpression, AT APS Composition and AT APS Condition
  • Examples for this Profile: ClinicalImpression/AtApsExampleClinicalImpression01
