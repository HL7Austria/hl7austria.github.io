Logical Model: Problem List (IPS)

Official URL: https://fhir.hl7.at/elga-iv-diabetes-r4/StructureDefinition/ProblemList-ips Version: 0.1.1
Draft as of 2025-07-07 Computable Name: ProblemListIps

Abbildung der Datenfelder, die für den Entwurf der Datenspezifikation des Rahmenkonzepts Integrierte Versorgung Diabetes Mellitus Typ 2 erforderlich sind, auf dem des IPS-Modul "Problem List".

Refer to the mapping from the logical model of diabetes to the logical model based on the International Patient Summary (IPS) in order to get an idea how the IPS relates to diabetes.

Usages:

  • This Logical Model is not used by any profiles in this Implementation Guide
