Resource Profile: AT ELGA e-Diagnose Condition

Official URL: https://fhir.hl7.at/elga/ediag/r4/StructureDefinition/at-elga-ediag-condition Version: 0.1.0
Active as of 2026-08-07 Responsible: ELGA GmbH Computable Name: AtEdiagCondition

Das AT e-Diagnose Condition-Profil leitet sich vom Condition-Profil ab und passt dieses für die Anforderungen der e-Diagnose an.

Usages:

  • Use this Profile: AT ELGA e-Diagnose Collection Bundle and AT ELGA e-Diagnose Transaction Bundle
  • Examples for this Profile: Condition/DiagnoseCurrentlyRelevantExample and Condition/DiagnoseNotCurrentlyRelevantExample

You can also check for usages in the FHIR IG Statistics
